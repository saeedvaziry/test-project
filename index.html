Why Muxy

Built for developers who hate waiting.

Every interaction is designed to disappear. No spinners, no jank, no warmup time. Open a session, split a pane, switch tabs — it just responds.

Native speed

Hand-tuned native code with zero runtime overhead. Boots in under 20ms and renders at 120 fps on ProMotion displays.

Effortless splits

Vertical, horizontal, nested — split panes with a single keystroke. Resize, swap, and zoom with smooth, predictable motion.

Persistent sessions

Reboot, close the lid, switch projects — your sessions stay exactly where you left them. Detach and reattach instantly.

Featherweight

A 4 MB binary that idles at under 12 MB of memory. No background daemons, no telemetry, no surprises.

Scriptable layouts

Define project layouts in a single YAML file. Open your editor, server, logs, and tests with one command.

macOS-first

Built around Apple's frameworks. Native fonts, native cursors, Touch ID for sudo, and full Apple Silicon optimization.

Performance

Numbers that feel like magic.

We benchmark against tmux and Wezterm on every commit. On an M3 Pro, Muxy outperforms both — and uses a fraction of the memory.
